Our Little Story 🐾

CozyCat Café was born from a simple dream: a place where every guest feels the warmth of home, surrounded by the gentle purring of happy cats.

We opened in 2018 with just three rescue cats — Mango, Ash, and the legendary Noodle. Today we have six fluffy residents who run the show (we just work for them).

All our cats are rescues. When you visit, you help support their care, vet visits, and the occasional dramatic 3am zoomies. 💕
